First release. 53 icons across AI, agents, MCP, and RAG, drawn in a single design
language: containers cut their corners at 45°, every shape fills the 2..22 live area,
and one 2px stroke throughout.

Ships `@iconmind/icons` (raw SVG, metadata, sprite) and `@iconmind/react` (one named
export per icon, ESM only, tree-shaken to roughly 500 bytes gzipped for a single icon).
