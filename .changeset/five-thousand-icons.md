Fifteen hundred icons and six domains: 3,787 → 5,287 icons, 31,722 drawings.

The new domains are travel, weather, health, education, home and social — the everyday half of a product that the AI half sits inside, 250 icons each. Every one is drawn to the same law as the rest: a declaration compiled into six cells, each cell gated on geometry, and the whole set rasterised afterwards so that no two icons render the same.

Three earlier rounds went back on the board before the new ones were drawn, because the contact sheets showed shapes fusing at 16 px rather than reading as objects: a guitar that read as a target on a post, a violin that read as a snowman, a still life that read as a face. The rule that came out of it holds across the set now — two shapes of the same visual weight need three units between them, or they become one.
